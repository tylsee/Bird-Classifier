Place one or more trained model checkpoints in this directory.

Supported filenames:
- resnet18_final.pth              (previous partial fine-tuning notebook)
- resnet18_head_only.pth          (combined notebook: frozen)
- resnet18_partial_finetune.pth   (combined notebook: partial)
- resnet18_full_finetune.pth      (combined notebook: full)

This template DOES NOT include trained model weights.
